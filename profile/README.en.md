# NepoPetro

### Collaborative portfolio of engineering, research, and multidisciplinary development

A shared workspace for researching, designing, developing, experimenting, and documenting engineering solutions through different disciplines, perspectives, and fields of knowledge.

---

## About this space

NepoPetro is a project ecosystem aimed at building a collaborative technical and scientific portfolio. Its purpose is to integrate knowledge, capabilities, and experiences from professionals, students, researchers, and people interested in developing engineering-oriented projects.

The portfolio is not limited to presenting final results. It also seeks to document the development process: the questions that originate each project, the hypotheses formulated, design decisions, methods used, experiments conducted, results obtained, and identified limitations.

Each project constitutes an independent research, development, or experimentation unit that can evolve on its own while also integrating with other initiatives within the ecosystem.

## Objectives

- Build a multidisciplinary portfolio of engineering and applied science projects.
- Promote collaboration among technical, scientific, and professional profiles.
- Integrate research, mathematical modeling, programming, data analysis, and technological development.
- Transform real-world problems into documented, reproducible, and assessable projects.
- Share methodologies, tools, knowledge, and experiences.
- Generate technical evidence of each contributor's work.
- Facilitate open collaboration, interdisciplinary learning, and knowledge transfer.
- Develop projects that may evolve into prototypes, products, research, publications, or practical solutions.

## Working methodology

We aim to ensure that projects follow an understandable, traceable, and technically grounded development process.

Depending on the nature and scope of each initiative, the process may include:

1. **Problem identification:** defining the context, needs, scope, and research questions.
2. **Research:** literature reviews, background, sources, foundations, and state of the art.
3. **Design:** architecture, methodology, modeling, hypotheses, and experimental planning.
4. **Development:** implementation of solutions, prototypes, tools, and systems.
5. **Validation:** testing, results analysis, quality assessment, and hypothesis verification.
6. **Documentation:** recording decisions, procedures, results, limitations, and evidence.
7. **Review:** technical assessment, interdisciplinary discussion, and improvement proposals.
8. **Evolution:** maintenance, expansion, new versions, and potential integrations with other projects.

Not every project needs to follow every stage or adhere to a rigid sequence. The methodology is adapted to the objectives, nature, and maturity of each initiative.

Collaboration may take place through:

## Principles

### Technical rigor

Claims, results, and conclusions should be grounded in evidence, appropriate methods, and identifiable sources.

### Reproducibility

Whenever feasible, experiments, procedures, and results should be reproducible by others.

### Transparency

We document decisions, assumptions, limitations, and methodological changes.

### Collaboration

We promote knowledge exchange, peer review, and collective development.

### Interdisciplinarity

We integrate different disciplines whenever problem complexity requires multiple perspectives.

### Continuous learning

The portfolio is also a space for education, experimentation, and professional development.

### Responsibility

We consider the technical, social, ethical, legal, and environmental implications of projects according to their context.

### Traceability

We seek to maintain a clear relationship between objectives, sources, decisions, processes, results, and contributions.
